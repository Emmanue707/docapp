Waghitech Docapp

Waghitech Docapp — your documents, on your device

Merge, split, sign, convert and OCR PDFs without sending files to the cloud. Android phone app plus desktop tools for Linux and Windows.

Download GitHub

What it does

  • PDF tools: merge, split, rotate, crop, reorder and stamp
  • PDF to Word with exact look or editable output
  • OCR and searchable PDFs for scans
  • Handwriting recognition (optional model download)
  • Visible and digital PDF signatures
  • Conversions between PDF, Word, ODT and images

Downloads

Get builds from the latest GitHub release:

  • Android universal APK
  • Linux x86_64 tarball (CLI + desktop + pdfium)
  • Windows x86_64 zip

Privacy

Processing stays on your phone or computer. OCR models download once and run locally. Nothing is uploaded for normal use.
